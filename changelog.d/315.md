### Changed — ADR 0002's in-container Codex cost claim is corrected against measurement (#315)

ADR 0002 costed the in-container Codex fix as "`CAP_SYS_ADMIN`, `--privileged`, or a
custom seccomp profile" and rejected that bundle as a standing security regression.
Measurement on 2026-08-04 (recorded in ADR 0013) falsified the premise: the gate is the
seccomp profile alone — `CAP_SYS_ADMIN` is neither sufficient (`pivot_root` is still
denied) nor required (`seccomp=unconfined` alone gives bwrap rc=0). ADR 0002 now carries
a dated amendment block with the measurement table and a resolvable link to ADR 0013,
and its Status line records that its premise was amended. It also corrects a second
consequence in its own text: "recoverable on the host when wanted" describes an act
nobody performs, since every tick runs through the wrapper.

What is corrected is the **cost**, not the **status**. Codex still degrades in-container
— the targeted profile is #314 and has not shipped — so no consumer prose flips to
"Codex works in-container", and the read-only `~/.codex` mount is explicitly not loosened
by the amendment.

The consumers that state the claim now name the correcting record: `CONTEXT.md`,
`README.md`, `agents/reviewer.md`, `commands/harness.md`, `docker/README.md`,
`specs/features/verb-model.md`, and the sibling ADRs 0001 and 0005.
`specs/architecture-principles.md` is corrected directly — it asserts *availability*
rather than location, so the derived search cannot reach it, and it is pinned by a
positional assertion instead.

The guard in `tests/unit/test_review_engine_decision.py` enforces the invariant over a
**derived** claim set — every paragraph of every normative markdown file that names Codex
and asserts where it runs — so a consumer added later is covered without being registered
anywhere. It demands a pointer, not a claim, so it keeps passing once #314 flips the
prose. Historical trees (`specs/proposals/`, `assessments/`, changelogs) are out of the
corpus by category: they record what was believed at a moment, not what is asserted now.
`harness/cli/doctor.py` encodes the same claim in remediation text and is deliberately
out of scope — #316 replaces it with a probe and guards it behaviourally.
