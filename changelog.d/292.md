### Changed — the one-ceiling rationale cites the measurement instead of a refuted premise (#292)

`CONTEXT.md`'s `engine_timeout_seconds` comment ended by calling the single
ceiling a mis-fit: the design engine "studies a whole worktree" and is
"systematically slower" than the review engine, which "reads a diff", so one
knob for two workloads was a defect awaiting a split.

That premise was never measured, and could not have been. ADR 0009 records that
`review` carried no duration until #264/#265, so the 600 → 720 retune saw only
design's distribution and assumed review's. Once both were on record the claim
did not survive: design leads by 45s at the median (366s vs 321s) and *trails*
at both q3 (447s vs 457s) and max (561s vs 587s) — and the tail is the only part
a timeout ceiling interacts with. `specs/proposals/per-engine-timeout-ceiling.md`
rejected the split on that evidence; the sentence it refuted was still sitting in
the one file every agent is told to read first.

The comment now states why one ceiling is right — one distribution with noise,
not two — quotes the tail pair, and cites the proposal as the record. The
ceiling itself is untouched at 720s.

The correction is guarded rather than hand-checked, in the module #291 already
built for this comment (`tests/unit/test_context_template_loop_block.py`) rather
than a parallel one. It takes the same two-step shape: the premise is **derived**
from the proposal's committed latency table, and only then is the prose required
not to contradict it and required to quote it. Neither half stands alone — a
derivation nothing reads goes green while the sentence rots, and a phrase pin
goes green on a reword.

The coupling runs both ways on purpose. A re-measurement that inverts the tail
fails the derivation, because `CONTEXT.md` and the rejection both rest on the
premise it would overturn; a proposal that is renamed, deleted, or flipped to
`accepted` fails the citation. The parse answers `{}` rather than raising when
the table moves, so a non-vacuity anchor asserts it first — otherwise a reshaped
table would let every derivation range over an empty dict and quietly stop
measuring.

Honest residual, stated in the test as it is for the sibling divergence guard: a
false speed claim phrased without any of the markers still passes the marker
check. It is the same class the ticket names, not all possible false prose.
