### Added — the interactive driver declares attendance, and a guard keeps every routine path out of it (#298)

`/harness run`'s prescribed `start` invocation now carries `--attended`, completing ADR 0011's opt-out: the one caller with an operator present declares it, and bounded stays the default everywhere else.

The half that keeps it honest is transitive. `/harness routine build` reaches `start` by handing the whole ticket to `/harness run`, so declaring the flag there would have handed every hourly tick an unbounded run — the failure the wall-clock breaker exists to prevent, arriving through the change meant to protect it. The routine preamble now states that no routine declares attendance whatever surface it drives, and the Build routine says so again at the point it delegates.

`tests/unit/test_attendance_declaration.py` derives both sets from the document's own headings, fence-aware, and asserts the obligation and the prohibition in both directions — including that a delegating routine declines in prose. It supersedes the interim #295 guards in `test_routine_commands.py`, whose hardcoded two-name list would have missed a third routine and whose substring predicate would have read the new declination sentences as the violation.
