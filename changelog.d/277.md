### Fixed — a failed `design` SUBMIT parse now records what would not parse (#277)

`harness design` fails its SUBMIT contract on 12.5% of attempts against
`review`'s 0.24% — the same engine, the same contract — and ADR 0007 D4 makes
the failure silent: the run degrades and ships without a design. The ledger
recorded only *that* the contract broke, so 72 attempts accumulated with nothing
to diagnose from.

A `no_submit` or `malformed_submit` event now carries two additive fields:
`submit_excerpt`, a bounded quote of the engine's own output, and
`stdout_chars`, its true length. The excerpt takes two windows — anchored at the
last `SUBMIT:` token and at the end of stdout — because the candidate causes show
up in different places: a payload spanning lines shows at the payload's start, a
truncation and a designed-but-never-submitted run at stdout's end. Bounded at
2,018 characters for stdout of any size; a real design payload measures 14–17 KB.

The other failure reasons (`engine_timeout`, `engine_error`, `no_ticket_spec`)
have no engine stdout to quote and stay absent, as does every successful design.
Nothing reaches stdout: the excerpt is for an operator reading the ledger, not
for the orchestrator's context.

**No fix to the failure rate itself yet, deliberately.** The prompt and the
parser are unchanged. Shipping a fix alongside the instrument would make the next
`harness stats` read unattributable — a lower rate could be either, and a flat
rate could be one masking the other. The next failure is now self-diagnosing,
which is what the fix should be chosen from.
