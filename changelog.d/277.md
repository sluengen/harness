### Fixed — a failed `design` SUBMIT parse now records what would not parse (#277)

`harness design` fails its SUBMIT contract on 12.5% of attempts against
`review`'s 0.24% — the same engine, the same contract — and the failure is
silent by design (ADR 0007 D4 degrades and the run ships without a design). The
ledger recorded only *that* the contract broke, so 72 attempts accumulated with
nothing to diagnose from.

A failed parse now carries a bounded excerpt of the offending output onto the
`design` event's `detail`: why it failed, the payload's true length, and a
head-and-tail window onto the text. Bounded at 400 characters — the ledger is an
audit trail, not a log, and a real design payload measures 14–17 KB.
