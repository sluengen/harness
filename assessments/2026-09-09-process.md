## Why this pass

Operation Nuke (`specs/proposals/operation-nuke.md`, items #620–#629) has landed in full. Item 9 owed a re-baselined assurance ratio; this is that report and nothing else. Scope is the three baseline rows and the comparison against the proposal's own revised projection. **No subtractive sweep was run and no deletion is proposed** — the rule the ticket attaches to AC-3 is that a projection that missed is a finding about the estimate, not a licence to delete more.

## Verdict

The retirement did roughly what it claimed. The unit suite halved — 27,841 lines across 56 modules down to 14,394 across 35 — and the assurance ratio fell from 6.86 to **2.78**, inside the ~2.8–3.1 band the proposal signed off against (`specs/proposals/operation-nuke.md:198`). Executables fell 60.2%, from 9,384 lines to 3,735. The whole-repo cross-check, which no glob choice can bend, is 61,139 → 36,982 tracked lines, **−39.5%**.

Two facts the headline hides matter more than it does. First, **the guidance denominator's composition changed underneath a command that did not change**: #626 moved the design system into `skills/design-system/assets/`, so 852 lines of markdown crossed *into* the guidance glob without anyone writing them, while the 380-line token builder moved *out of* every glob and is now counted nowhere. The series figure therefore flatters the result — like-for-like, against a denominator of 5,178 − 852 = 4,326, the ratio is **3.33, above the band, not inside it**. Both readings are true of the same tree; this report picks neither. Second, the three modules the proposal classed as "wording guards over prose" (`:202`) — the last open band, ~1,340 projected lines, 1,436 actual — are **not wording guards**. Probed in both directions, all three fail a real divergence and pass a consistent reword: **0 / 3 vacuous**. The optimistic end of the old band assumed lines that turn out to be load-bearing.
