## Approach: what to preserve

The architectural direction fits a pre-user tool. The fix lane removes a ticket, design and reviewer from genuinely small work; heavier lanes spend more only when a contract or protected area warrants it. Configuration has one reader, the builder is normally the orchestrator, and landing binds evidence to authored bytes. Native host facilities carry installation, isolation and agent dispatch. These choices reduce handoffs and duplicate state without making the agent's confidence the landing authority (P0–P3).

The bounded queue, explicit hold and finite review loop also have the right purpose. Their remaining weakness is execution of the judgment steps: bad intake can still fill the bounded queue, and a written refusal does not establish that an agent followed it. This pass reproduced that distinction in the cleanup guard and observed it in the latest drain. The next improvement should shrink that rework before adding more rules.
